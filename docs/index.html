Foly Ananou

Researcher · Writer · Economist

Foly Ananou is a researcher at Société Générale and an academic economist working on financial markets, banking structure, and monetary integration in West Africa. His research focuses on the WAEMU and CEMAC monetary unions, sovereign debt markets, and pan-African banking networks. He writes in French and English on African economic policy, financial inclusion, and institutional design.
