Foly Ananou

PhD Economist

Foly Ananou is a financial economist — which is a polite way of saying he spends his days asking whether banks and governments can actually pay their bills, and why money behaves the way it does. He works on banking, sovereign risk, and the quiet machinery that keeps financial systems standing (or doesn't). When the data gets interesting he writes about it, in French and English, and he is slowly turning a pile of notes called Dakar days into a book.
